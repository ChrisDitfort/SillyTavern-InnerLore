World Progression Engine
A private chronologist reads each completed player/story exchange, estimates elapsed fictional time, and updates goals, processes, deadlines, and restrained future beats. Simulated events remain private until narration reveals them, and player-required actions are never completed automatically.
Automatic Event Director (optional) Off by default. When the event pool needs another beat, a focused background call reads the committed SQLite/graph state and proposes at most one source-cited event. It never runs while disabled or while an event awaits delivery.
